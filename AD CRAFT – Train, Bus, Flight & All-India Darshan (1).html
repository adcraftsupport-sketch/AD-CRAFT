Yatri Suchi aur Ticket Booking

Yatri Suchi (ek ticket par max 6)

5 saal se kam bachche ka ticket nahi lagta (berth nahi milti); 5 saal ya usse upar ke bachche ka ticket lena hota hai. Naam bilkul ID jaisa likhein. Tatkal mein ID ki jaankari alag se li jayegi. Saved suchi ke liye Login karein.

Mera Account (Login)

Yeh login sirf aapke is phone/browser mein kaam karta hai. Ismein aapka naam, mobile aur saved yatri isi device par rakhe jaate hain, kisi server par nahi. Browser data saaf karne par ya doosre phone par yeh nahi milega. Isliye PIN mein apna bank PIN ya password na rakhein.

Festival & Summer Special Trains

Tyohar aur garmi ki chhuttiyon mein Railway alag special trains chalati hai. Inme seat jaldi bharti hai, isliye pehle se jaankari aur booking karein.

Chhath Special

Oct–Nov. Mumbai, Delhi, Gujarat, Punjab se Bihar, Jharkhand aur Purvi UP ki taraf sabse zyada bheed.

Diwali Special

Oct–Nov. Metro cities se ghar jaane wale yatri; Diwali se 10–15 din pehle booking bharti hai.

Summer Special

Apr–Jun. School chhuttiyan, Vaishno Devi, Char Dham, Shimla-Manali aur ghar wapsi ke routes.

Holi Special

Mar. UP, Bihar, Rajasthan aur MP ke routes par extra trains.

Durga Puja / Dussehra

Sep–Oct. West Bengal, Odisha, Assam aur Jharkhand routes.

Mela / Kumbh / Yatra Special

Prayagraj, Haridwar, Ujjain, Puri Rath Yatra jaise bade melon ke liye.

Shaadi & Winter Season

Nov–Feb. Group travel aur New Year trips ke liye extra demand.

Dhyan rakhein: Special trains ka time-table aur number Railway announce karti hai, aksar 2–4 hafte pehle. Inka fare aur booking rules normal train se alag ho sakte hain. Zaroori Travel Guidelines

Train yatra

Original photo ID rakhein (Aadhaar, Voter ID, Passport, Driving Licence). E-ticket ya SMS ke saath ID dikhani hoti hai. Station par 45–60 minute pehle pahunchein. Platform aur coach ki jaankari chart ban jaane ke baad dobara check karein. Qeemti saman aur dawai apne paas rakhein.

Flight yatra

Domestic mein 2 ghante aur International mein 3 ghante pehle airport pahunchein. Naam bilkul ID/Passport jaisa hona chahiye. Web check-in, baggage limit aur cabin saman ke niyam airline ke hisaab se dekhein. International ke liye passport kam se kam 6 mahine valid hona chahiye aur visa zaroori hai.

Bus yatra

Boarding point aur bus ka number pehle confirm karein. Raat ki yatra mein apna saman seat ke paas rakhein. Pahadi routes par mausam ke karan time badal sakta hai.

Bachche aur Senior Citizens

Bachchon ke age ka proof (Aadhaar/birth certificate) saath rakhein. Senior citizens apni dawai, doctor ki salah aur emergency contact saath rakhein. Chadhai wali yatra mein dheere chalein aur beech mein aaram karein.

Mahilayein aur Akele yatri

Raat ki yatra mein hotel/pickup ki jaankari kisi apne ko share karein. Anjaan logon se khane-peene ki cheez na lein. Registered cab/taxi hi use karein.

Sehat aur Safai

Saaf paani piyein aur bahar ka kachcha khana avoid karein. Pahadon par oxygen kam hone se sar dard ya ghabrahat ho to turant rukkar doctor ko dikhayein. Apni zaroori dawai pura stock rakhein.

Mandir aur Tirth maryada

Jooton aur mobile ke liye locker ka istemal karein. Prasad, dakshina aur puja ke liye sirf adhikrit counter par bhugtan karein. Kisi bhi dalal ya anjaan vyakti ko paise na dein.

Paise aur Saman ki suraksha

Zyada cash na rakhein, UPI/card ka upyog karein. ID ki photocopy aur digital copy alag rakhein. Bheed mein bag aage rakhein.

Documents Checklist

Photo ID, ticket/voucher, hotel booking, dawai ki parchi, emergency contact number, aur International ke liye passport, visa aur travel insurance.

Aksar Puche Jaane Wale Sawal

Booking kaise hoti hai?

Form bharein ya WhatsApp karein. Hum seat/availability check karke aapko batate hain, phir aapki sehmati par booking hoti hai.

Waitlist ticket milega?

Nahi, hum sirf Confirmed, Tatkal aur RAC booking karte hain.

Special train ka fare normal jaisa hoga?

Zaroori nahi. Special trains ke fare aur niyam Railway tay karti hai, isliye booking se pehle hum aapko exact fare batate hain.

Cancel karne par refund kitna milega?

Hamari policy: Refund = Total Amount minus 25%. Railway/airline/hotel ke alag charge lag sakte hain.

Senior citizens ke liye suvidha hai?

Haan. Dheemi gati ka plan, kam chadhai wale rasta, wheelchair/doli aur nazdeeki hotel ki vyavastha poochh sakte hain.

Videsh se parivar ke liye last rites mein madad?

Haan. Airport pickup, hotel, cab, pandit coordination aur asthi visarjan mein sahayata di jati hai.
